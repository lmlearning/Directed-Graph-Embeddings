# Paper Summary: HOPE (Asymmetric Transitivity Preserving Graph Embedding)

## Bibliographic Information
- **Paper Title:** Asymmetric Transitivity Preserving Graph Embedding
- **Authors:** Mingdong Ou, Peng Cui, Jian Pei, Ziwei Zhang, Wenwu Zhu
- **Venue/Year:** KDD 2016

## High-Level Summary
**Core Idea:** HOPE addresses the fundamental challenge that existing graph embedding methods cannot preserve asymmetric transitivity in directed graphs. The method learns two embedding vectors per node (source and target) that approximate high-order proximity measures through a scalable generalized SVD approach, achieving O(m·K²·L) time complexity and significantly outperforming baselines on graph reconstruction, link prediction, and vertex recommendation tasks.
